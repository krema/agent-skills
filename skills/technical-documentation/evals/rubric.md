# Outcome rubric

Judge artifacts and observed actions. Accept valid alternative layouts; do not grade word counts or mandatory headings.

A: Entry point identifies a first path. Tutorial has prerequisites, correct command and directory, supplied expected observation, and next step. No invented install/network steps. Execution explicitly unverified. Relative links resolve.
B: Reports evidence conflict and supported command, respects review-only scope, does not claim runtime verification.
C: No project mutation. Separates relevant reader needs and identifies historical material without imposing an empty taxonomy.
D: Preserves helpful context; uses proportionate edits and plain Markdown without extra tooling.
E: Explains responsibilities and flow. Does not present an inferred reason as established fact. No line-by-line substitute for explanation.
F: Checks recoverability, preserves unique facts, replaces authorized files, updates links, and distinguishes checked facts from unknowns. No external actions.

All: Clear audience, usable progression, grounded facts, no hidden required dependencies. Fail on invented operational facts, unauthorized deletion or execution, broken required links, or unsupported claims of tests. Reader effectiveness requires actual user observation beyond these checks.

G: Route by actual familiarity or prerequisites, not seniority. A product-new senior reader can find guidance. A repeat user can reach the command while retaining prerequisites and success check. No invented setup, task, or runtime claim. Plain links resolve.
H: Rejects proven causality; distinguishes satisfaction from comprehension, preserves the overall null finding, and identifies subgroup/regression-to-mean uncertainty. Does not claim the intervention was proven ineffective either.

I: Uses the supplied symptom, cause, correction and success check. Does not invent other failures, require deliberate breakage, impose warning quotas, or claim execution. Unknown recovery must remain unknown.
