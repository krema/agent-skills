# B — Research worker audit

Retain the coordinator with bounded regional research workers for the next comparable request. The three regional partitions are supported by the inspected task structure: adaptive search and conflicting publication dates require judgment, the regions have no identified dependencies or shared mutable writes, and each investigation produces much more material than synthesis needs. A deterministic lookup tool would not cover the described investigation.

Give each region a bounded assignment containing the shared product definitions, availability-date interpretation, region, evidence requirements, read-only effects, budget, and completion condition. Use concurrent execution where the available budget permits; three regions justify three assignments, not a universal worker-count rule. Require a result with completion or partial status, findings, the specified product/region/availability-date/source-URL/source-date/conflict fields, and what was actually inspected. Preserve exact dates and source identifiers, and explicitly return missing data and unresolved publication-date contradictions. A timeout remains partial.

The coordinator owns the user conversation and cross-region synthesis. It should inspect the decisive cited evidence, particularly contradictions and consequential availability claims, before relying on a summary. Bulky search intermediates can stay in each worker's context; evidence rows and unresolved issues must cross the boundary.

The observations support continuing this existing arrangement, not a demonstrated speedup. The three pilot traces show no duplicate searches; that is limited coverage. Parallel runs took 4–5 minutes and older serial runs 11–13 minutes, but the requests differed, model versions were unrecorded, and token totals were absent. Neither a causal architecture benefit nor a cost saving can be inferred.

Before changing the architecture, compare comparable requests with matched inputs, models/settings, tools, budget, and outcome grading. Check coverage, date correctness, citation support, contradiction handling, and synthesis loss; collect wall-clock time and total calls, tokens, and cost across coordinator and workers, including retries and failures. Repeated handbacks, new cross-region dependencies, duplicated investigation, or evidence omitted during synthesis would justify testing a merged/shared loop. A genuinely standardized lookup could justify a tool. Retain regional isolation if matched evidence shows its context savings or completed-task performance earns its coordination cost. No workers were launched and no new research was performed.
